Bill your personal workspace instead of your own account. Omni bills workspaces, and your personal workspace stands in for you, so a plan bought on the Omni account hub for your personal workspace now unlocks Beacon (voice, unlimited conversations, the paid default model, and its included credits), and managed AI draws on that workspace's credits. A new `GET /api/billing/plan` endpoint reports that workspace's plan and credit balance.
